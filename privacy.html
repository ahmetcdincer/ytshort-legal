Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

ytshort ("the app") is a private, self-hosted tool operated by a single channel owner to publish short gaming videos to their own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • Google account authorization (OAuth tokens) — used only to upload videos to, and read basic information (channel ID, title, thumbnail) about, the YouTube channel you connect.
  • Video metadata you create — titles, descriptions, tags and scheduling times for videos you approve for upload.

The app requests the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. It does not access your email, contacts, or any data from other Google services.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used solely to upload and schedule videos on the connected channel.
  • OAuth refresh tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own server and are never shared with third parties.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.
  • The app does not use cookies or tracking for visitors of this website.

Retention and deletion

Stored tokens are deleted when the channel is disconnected in the app. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
